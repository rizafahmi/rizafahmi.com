# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Tech-focused professionals, developers, and designers visiting to read articles, learn Elixir/AI, or explore personal/open-source projects. They appreciate strong visual craft, high-contrast typography, and digital layouts that feel like art.

## Product Purpose
A personal site, blog, and portfolio for Riza Fahmi (Co-Founder of Hacktiv8, Elixir & AI enthusiast) that serves as a bold educational resource, an open-source project showcase, and an artistic reflection of digital craft.

## Brand Personality
Energetic, brutalist, artistic, high-contrast, and unapologetically modern. Evokes raw engineering and creative energy through massive typography, striking color blocks, and structured asymmetry rather than safe corporate templates.

## Anti-references
- Low-contrast slate-on-grey "Notion-like" minimal aesthetics
- SaaS templates (identical card grids, side-stripes, text gradients)
- Floating glassmorphism cards and soft shadow elevations
- Neon cyberpunk clichés (neon-on-black terminal look)

## Design Principles
- **Brutalist Asymmetry**: Break standard alignments. Offset sections, use unequal spacing, and employ stark geometric borders to create tension and rhythm.
- **Solarized Energy**: High-chroma color drenching (Electric Cobalt & Acid Lime) on top of raw concrete light mode or deep obsidian dark mode. Color is structure, not decoration.
- **Typographic Scale**: Enormous, wide headlines paired with structured editorial body copy to establish immediate typographic presence.
- **Tactile Interactions**: Hover actions should feel alive, filling entire cards or blocks with solid contrast colors instead of tiny text color shifts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Respect user reduced-motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`).
- Strict WCAG AA contrast compliance: text content must maintain high contrast (at least 4.5:1 for body text) using our cobalt-black-white color system.
- Focus states and keyboard-navigable interactive controls with thick, high-visibility outlines.
