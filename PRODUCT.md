# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Tech-focused professionals, developers, and students visiting to read articles, learn Elixir, tech productivity, or view personal projects. They often read in dim lighting/dark mode contexts.

## Product Purpose
A personal site, blog, and portfolio for Riza Fahmi (Co-Founder of Hacktiv8, Elixir & AI enthusiast) that serves as a high-quality educational resource, a showcase for personal/open-source projects, and a reflection of personal craft.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, editorial-typographic, curious, expert, and clean. Evokes tech expertise and curiosity through refined layout, subtle motion, and clear visual structure rather than generic tech clichés.

## Anti-references
- Neon on black (generic tech/cyberpunk themes)
- Dry, overly corporate, or template-like layouts
- SaaS clichés (identical card grids, side-stripes, text gradients)

## Design Principles
- **Editorial Precision**: Focus on typography hierarchy and layout rhythm rather than decorative frames or cards.
- **Craft Over Cliché**: Reject generic tech/AI aesthetic tropes (no neon-on-black or glow-effects). Use restrained, tinted OKLCH neutrals.
- **Intended Dark-First**: Optimize for low-light reading with a premium dark-first palette that meets strict WCAG AA contrast standards.
- **Micro-Delight**: Use subtle, custom, non-layout-shifting animations (like custom typewriter or smooth link underlines) to engage the reader without distraction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG AA/AAA color contrast compliance for text readability.
- Respect user reduced-motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`).
- Focus states and keyboard-navigable interactive controls.
