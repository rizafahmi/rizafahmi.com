### Anti-Patterns Verdict
**PASS.** The homepage successfully adheres to the "Minimalist Foundry" Creative North Star. All previous Tells—such as emoji headers, duplicate styles, fuzzy bold stroke effects, spaced em dashes, and sequential numbered header markers—have been resolved.

### Overall Impression
The homepage has a strong developer character and benefits from excellent typography fallbacks, a responsive grid, and static resilience. The interface presents a premium, typographic-first layout with high visual hierarchy and clear readability.

### What's Working
1. **Typographic Headings**: Plain typographic section headers establish clean, visual hierarchy.
2. **Minimal Content Density**: Article lists are capped at a maximum of 3 items, reducing cognitive scanning effort.
3. **Accessibility Safeguards**: Sized up touch targets and skip-links protect accessibility users.
